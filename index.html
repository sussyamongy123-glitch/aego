Server Rules

Welcome to the wild. Here are the few guidelines we enforce:

  • Stealing & Raiding Allowed: Bases are fair game. Lock up your loot or risk losing it to clever thieves.
  • Open PvP & Traps: Non-consensual combat, ambushes, and deadly traps are fully permitted anywhere outside safe zones. Trust no one.
  • No Hacking or Exploits: Using hacked clients, X-ray texture packs, duping exploits, or unfair advantages will result in an instant ban.
  • Be Respectful in Chat: Zero tolerance for hate speech, harassment, severe toxicity, or spamming global channels. Keep it civil out-of-game.
